Todo Planner Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026 · App: Todo Planner (Android, package com.samuelbigley.todoplanner) · Developer: Samuel Bigley

Todo Planner lets you plan your Microsoft To Do tasks into your days and, if you choose, add the plan to your Outlook calendar. This policy explains what the app accesses, where that information goes, and the choices you have.

In short: the app talks directly from your device to Microsoft. The developer runs no servers, does not receive your data, and does not sell, share or analyse it. There are no ads, analytics or tracking.

Signing in

You sign in with a Microsoft account (work, school or personal) through Microsoft's own sign-in page or the Microsoft Authenticator / Company Portal app. The app never sees your password. Microsoft gives the app access tokens, which are kept in your device's secure storage (Android Keystore) and used only to call Microsoft on your behalf. You can sign in to several accounts; each account's tokens are stored separately.

Information the app accesses

With your permission, the app uses Microsoft Graph to access the following data in the accounts you sign in to:

  • Basic profile (User.Read): your name and email address, to show which account is signed in.
  • Microsoft To Do tasks (Tasks.ReadWrite): your task lists and open tasks, including titles, notes, steps, importance, due dates and attachments. The app reads them to show and plan them, and writes back only the changes you make (for example editing a title, ticking a step, completing a task or attaching a photo).
  • Outlook calendar (Calendars.ReadWrite), only if you turn on calendar sync: the app creates, moves and deletes events for the tasks you plan. It only changes events it created, which are tagged “Todo Planner”.
  • Photos and camera, only when you choose to attach a photo to a task: the photo you pick or take is uploaded to that task in your Microsoft To Do. The app does not otherwise access your photos or camera.

Information stored on your device

To work offline between syncs, the app keeps the following on your device only: your plan (which task is scheduled when), your settings (work hours, break, planning options), task lengths you set, and the link between each planned task and its calendar event. This information is not sent to the developer or anyone else.

How data is shared

Your data travels only between your device and Microsoft, over encrypted HTTPS connections, and is stored by Microsoft under your account and Microsoft's own privacy statement. The developer does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your personal data, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Sign out of an account in Settings to remove that account's tokens from the device.
  • Uninstall the app to delete everything it stored on the device.
  • Revoke access at any time: for personal accounts at account.live.com/consent/Manage; for work or school accounts at myapps.microsoft.com or through your organisation's administrator.
  • Calendar events the app created are ordinary events in your calendar; turning calendar sync off removes upcoming ones, and you can delete any of them in Outlook.

Work and school accounts

If you sign in with an account provided by your organisation, your organisation's administrator may control whether the app can be used and may see that it has been approved. Your organisation's own policies apply to that account's data.

Children

Todo Planner is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed to collect information from them.

Security

Sign-in uses Microsoft's standard OAuth 2.0 flow with PKCE, tokens are kept in the device's secure storage, and all network traffic is encrypted. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but the app is built to keep your data on your device and in your Microsoft account only.
